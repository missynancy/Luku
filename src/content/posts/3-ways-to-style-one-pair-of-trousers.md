If you've been following the capsule wardrobe series, you already know the rule: buy pieces that work hard, not pieces that only work once.

This post puts that rule into practice. One pair of black tailored trousers, three completely different outfits — proof that a well-chosen piece can flex across contexts without you needing to buy more.

## Look One — Polished Office

Pair the trousers with a crisp white blouse and a matching camel blazer, finished with a structured tote and tan pointed-toe heels. This is the buttoned-up, client-ready version — ideal for meetings, presentations, or any day you need to look put-together without overthinking it.

## Look Two — Smart Casual

Swap the blazer for nothing at all, or wear it open over a simple top, and let the trousers do the work. Add loafer-style heels and a smaller everyday bag. This version trades formality for ease — perfect for a lighter office day, a lunch meeting, or running errands after work.

## Look Three — Elevated Weekend

Pair the same trousers with a fitted cream corset top and a relaxed shoulder bag, finished with simple white sneakers. This look feels elevated without feeling like "office wear" — great for weekend brunch, a casual event, or dressing up a low-key day out.

## Why This Matters

This isn't just a styling exercise — it's the actual test we recommended in the [10-piece capsule wardrobe guide](https://missynancy.github.io/Luku/posts/smart-office-outfit-guide-kenyan-professionals): before buying anything new, ask whether it can work with at least three things you already own.

One pair of well-chosen trousers, styled three ways, means you spent once and got three outfits back. That's the entire logic behind a capsule wardrobe — fewer purchases, more mileage.

> **SmartLuku Style Tip**
> Before your next purchase, picture it in at least three outfits. If you can't, it's not earning its place in your closet yet.
