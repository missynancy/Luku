Eating healthy in Kenya doesn't require a big budget or imported ingredients. Local markets and supermarkets are already full of foods that are both nutritious and affordable. Here's a complete list to guide your next shopping trip.

## Protein Sources

- **Eggs** — one of the cheapest complete proteins available, versatile for any meal
- **Ndengu (mung beans)** — high in protein and fiber, and filling for hours
- **Omena** — small dried fish, packed with protein and healthy fats
- **Beans** — affordable, easy to store, and a staple in most Kenyan kitchens
- **Minji (peas)** — a good stew base with solid protein content
- **Kamande (lentils)** — quick to cook and gentle on the budget
- **Chicken** — best bought in bulk and frozen in portions to stretch your spending

## Vegetables

- **Sukuma wiki** — one of the cheapest, most versatile greens available
- **Managu** — a nutrient-dense traditional vegetable
- **Cabbage** — long shelf life and budget-friendly
- **Spinach** — easy to find and simple to prepare
- **Tomatoes and onions** — the base of most Kenyan cooking, cheap and always in season

## Carbohydrates

- **Ugali flour (brown or white)** — a filling staple; pair with a protein source for a balanced meal
- **Sweet potatoes** — naturally filling and nutrient-rich
- **Brown rice** — a slightly pricier but healthier alternative to white rice
- **Oats** — an affordable, quick breakfast option

## Fruits

- **Bananas** — cheap, filling, and widely available
- **Mangoes** (in season) — naturally sweet and nutrient-dense
- **Watermelon** — hydrating and budget-friendly, especially in season

## Fats and Extras

- **Groundnuts** — a healthy, affordable snack or meal addition
- **Plain yogurt** — a versatile protein and probiotic source

## How to Build a Healthy Plate from This List

A simple rule: fill half your plate with vegetables, a quarter with a protein source, and a quarter with a carbohydrate like ugali, rice, or sweet potato. Using the foods above, a full day of eating can easily stay under a modest daily budget.
