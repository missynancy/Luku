## Protein content in omena

A serving of dried omena provides a strong amount of protein for its size, which is why it is such a valuable staple in many Kenyan homes. It is especially useful for people who want more protein without paying for imported powders or expensive meat cuts. In practical terms, omena can help you hit your daily protein targets while keeping food costs low.

For bodybuilders or anyone focused on muscle gain, protein quality matters, but consistency matters even more. Omena fits well into a high-protein diet because it is easy to add to porridge, stew, rice, ugali, and vegetable dishes. It is not just a cheap protein source; it is a convenient one.

## A simple omena recipe

### Omena stew with tomatoes and greens

Ingredients:
- One cup of dried omena
- 1 tomato, chopped
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 cups of cooked greens or sukuma wiki
- 1 tablespoon of oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional: chili, garlic, or a little ginger

Method:
1. Rinse the omena lightly to remove excess salt and grit.
2. Fry the onion in a little oil until soft.
3. Add the tomatoes and cook until they break down into a sauce.
4. Stir in the omena and cook for a few minutes until it is well mixed.
5. Add the greens and season to taste.
6. Serve with rice, ugali, or sweet potatoes.

This is a simple, budget-friendly way to turn omena into a proper meal that still supports muscle growth.

## Tips for reducing bitterness

Some people find omena a little bitter at first, especially if it is not rinsed well or if it is overcooked. The easiest fix is to rinse it thoroughly before frying or cooking, and to balance it with tomatoes, onions, garlic, or a little chili. A quick roast or light fry also brings out a richer taste and helps reduce the sharp bitterness.
