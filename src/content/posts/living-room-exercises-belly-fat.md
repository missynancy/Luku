## Start with a simple routine

You do not need fancy equipment to move your body and improve your fitness. A few effective exercises can be done in a living room with a timer and a bit of discipline.

### The 5 exercises

1. Bodyweight squats
2. Mountain climbers
3. Glute bridges
4. Standing knee raises
5. Dead bug

## How to build consistency

Try 3 rounds of 30 seconds work and 20 seconds rest. Repeat 3 times a week and pair it with better food choices for stronger results.
