## Start with a simple routine

A simple living room workout you can do to target belly fat — no gym, no equipment, just consistency. You do not need fancy equipment to move your body and improve your fitness. These five exercises work your core, raise your heart rate, and can all be done on a mat, rug, or bare floor.

### The 5 exercises

**1. Bodyweight Squats — 3 x 15**
- Builds full-body strength and raises your heart rate, which supports fat loss overall
- Engages your core to stay balanced through each rep

**2. Mountain Climbers — 3 x 20 (each leg)**
- Doubles as cardio and core work, making it one of the most efficient moves in this routine
- Keeps your abs braced the entire time, directly targeting belly fat

**3. Glute Bridges — 3 x 15**
- Activates your glutes and lower back, which supports better posture during ab work
- Strengthens your core from a different angle than standing exercises

**4. Standing Knee Raises — 3 x 20 (each side)**
- Targets your lower abs and hip flexors without any floor work needed
- Easy to do during a desk break — no need to change clothes or find floor space

**5. Dead Bug — 3 x 12 (each side)**
- Trains deep core stability, the muscles that flatten and tighten your midsection over time
- Low-impact and beginner-friendly, so it's safe to do daily

## How to build consistency

Try 3 rounds of 30 seconds work and 20 seconds rest for each exercise. Repeat this routine 3 times a week and pair it with better food choices for stronger results.
