Snacking doesn't have to mean reaching for expensive packaged foods or something that ruins your progress. These five snacks are cheap, quick to prepare, and actually keep you full until your next meal.

## 1. Boiled Eggs

Boil a few eggs at the start of the week and keep them in the fridge. One or two eggs make a filling, protein-rich snack that takes seconds to grab.

## 2. Roasted Groundnuts

A small handful of roasted groundnuts is filling, affordable, and easy to carry with you for a snack on the go.

## 3. Plain Yogurt with a Banana

Plain yogurt paired with a sliced banana gives you protein, natural sweetness, and a bit of fiber, all for a low cost.

## 4. Boiled Sweet Potato

A small boiled sweet potato is naturally filling and works well as a mid-afternoon snack when you need something more substantial than a handful of nuts.

## 5. Roasted Maize (Mahindi Choma)

A classic, affordable street snack. Roasted maize is filling, easy to find, and a much better option than reaching for chips or a sugary snack.

## Making Snacking Work for You

The key to healthy snacking on a budget is preparation. Boil a batch of eggs or sweet potatoes at the start of the week so you always have something ready, rather than reaching for whatever's convenient in the moment.
