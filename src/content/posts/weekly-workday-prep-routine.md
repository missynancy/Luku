Most Monday morning stress isn't caused by a heavy workload. It's caused by making too many small, avoidable decisions before you even step out the door.

When you're trying to figure out what outfit to wear, looking for matching shoes, deciding when you'll squeeze in movement, and rushing to get ready for work, your energy is drained before the workday starts.

A simple **Sunday prep routine** changes the pace of your entire week. You don't need an elaborate three-hour routine — just 30 intentional minutes on Sunday afternoon or evening.

---

## 1. Plan 3 Outfits Ahead of Time

Instead of standing in front of your closet every morning wondering what to wear, choose and prepare your key work outfits for the week.

- **Check your schedule:** Identify high-stakes days (client meetings, presentations) that call for formal corporate wear versus lighter days that fit smart casual outfits.
- **Inspect details:** Ensure shirts are ironed, trousers are ready, and shoes are clean.
- **Hang them together:** Place complete outfit combinations (blouse, blazer, trousers, accessories) on dedicated hangers.

> **Why it works:** Eliminating morning outfit decisions saves 15–20 minutes of friction every morning and ensures you step out looking polished and put-together.

---

## 2. Block 30-Minute Movement Windows

If you wait until you have "free time" for fitness during the workweek, it usually doesn't happen. Treat your movement sessions like non-negotiable appointments on your calendar.

- Pick **3 specific days** (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday morning or evening).
- Set a **30-minute boundary** — whether it's a home core workout, balcony cardio session, or a workday mobility break.
- Lay out your workout clothes next to your bed or work bag the night before.

---

## 3. Set Up Your Morning Launchpad

Designate one spot near your door for everyday work essentials:
- Work bag & laptop charger
- House keys & ID badge
- Water bottle & commute essentials

Getting into the habit of dropping these items in the exact same launchpad spot on Sunday night prevents the frantic 10-minute search for keys on Monday morning.

---

## 4. Conduct a 10-Minute Desk & Calendar Reset

Spend 10 minutes looking at the week ahead:
- Review your primary work deliverables for the week.
- Identify your **Top 2 priorities** for Monday so you can start focused work immediately.
- Clear clutter from your workspace or home desk.

---

## 5. Define Your Evening Wind-Down Boundary

Workday energy depends on rest quality. Establish a clear transition point between work mode and personal time:
- Set an alarm for 30 minutes before bedtime to turn off work emails and screens.
- Spend 5 minutes reflecting or jotting down remaining tasks for the next day to clear your mind.

---

## Sustainable Habits over Perfect Routines

You don't need perfection to feel organized. Starting with just two of these habits this Sunday will give you noticeable momentum and clarity when Monday morning arrives.
